## Production Notes

### Software Recommendations
- **Screen Recording:** OBS Studio (free), Camtasia, ScreenFlow
- **Video Editing:** DaVinci Resolve (free), Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro
- **Animation:** After Effects, Keynote/PowerPoint (for simple animations)
- **Music:** Epidemic Sound, Artlist, YouTube Audio Library

### Recording Checklist
- [ ] Set screen resolution to 1920x1080
- [ ] Close all unnecessary applications
- [ ] Disable desktop notifications
- [ ] Use test data (not real customer data)
- [ ] Record at 60fps for smooth playback
- [ ] Use high-quality microphone for voiceover
- [ ] Record in quiet environment

### Editing Guidelines
- **Transitions:** Keep simple (cross-dissolve, 0.5s duration)
- **Text:** Use brand colors (primary: #667eea)
- **Animations:** Smooth, not too fast
- **Music:** 80% volume, fade in/out
- **Voiceover:** Clear, conversational tone
- **Pacing:** Not too fast, allow time to absorb

### Export Settings
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps or 60fps
- **Codec:** H.264 (MP4)
- **Bitrate:** 10-15 Mbps (high quality)
- **Audio:** AAC, 192kbps, Stereo

### Distribution
- **YouTube:** Upload as unlisted/public
- **Website:** Embed on landing page
- **Social Media:** Create 30s teaser versions
- **Email:** Include thumbnail with play button

## Voice Talent Guidelines

If hiring voiceover artist:

**Voice Type:**
- Gender: Neutral preference (either works well)
- Age: 30-45 range
- Accent: Neutral (American, British, or Australian)
- Tone: Friendly, professional, confident

**Direction:**
- Speak clearly but conversationally
- Smile while speaking (it shows in voice)
- Emphasize benefits, not just features
- Natural pace, not rushed
- Pause slightly at text overlays

**Sample Lines to Test:**
1. "Introducing Shop Manager - the complete retail management system designed for small businesses like yours."
2. "Process sales lightning-fast with our intuitive point-of-sale system."
3. "Ready to simplify your retail management? Download Shop Manager today."
